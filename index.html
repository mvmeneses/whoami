Who I Am

About Me

I’m a licensed Electronics Engineer (ECE) and currently the NOC Manager at Nexlogic Telecommunications Network Inc., a Philippines-based ISP and Tech Solutions Company with GPON/FTTH infrastructure across Bulacan, Pampanga, Cavite, and Manila.

With 15+ years in ISP and telecom operations, I manage everything from BGP border routing with peerings to different ISPs and Internet Exchanges in Philippines, Singapore and Hongkong, to FTTH provisioning, Linux infrastructure, FreeRADIUS AAA, and internal tooling. I also consult for cable operators and SMEs through my own practice.

I’m an IECEP member based in Zambales. My current focus is building better operational tools, improving NOC observability, and automating ISP workflows.
